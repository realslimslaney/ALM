**Whole life (WL) insurance** pays a fixed death benefit whenever the insured dies, funded by
level premiums for life. The reference page solves the net level premium by the equivalence
principle on SOA 2012 IAM mortality, shows premium and benefit cash flows from the insurer's
side, and measures rate sensitivity of the net liability with DV01 (its PV is near zero, so
Macaulay duration is not used). It is an executable Quarto page (`ref-whole-life.qmd`) rendered
on the docs site; this stub redirects to it.
