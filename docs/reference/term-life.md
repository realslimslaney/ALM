**Term life insurance** pays a death benefit only if the insured dies within a fixed term, so
premiums are far lower than whole life and the liability runs off at the end of the term. The
reference page builds a term policy on SOA 2012 IAM mortality, shows its net cash flows,
compares term lengths and a whole life policy for the same insured, and measures rate
sensitivity with DV01. It is an executable Quarto page (`ref-term-life.qmd`) rendered on the
docs site; this stub redirects to it.
