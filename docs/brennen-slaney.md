## About the speaker

*As of the lecture, February 2026.*

**Brennen Slaney, FSA** is an actuary who moved from health insurance into life and annuity
reinsurance, where asset-liability management became a daily concern.

- **University of Alabama**, 2016 to 2019: BS in Finance and MS in Applied Statistics. Grew up in
  Cincinnati and lived in Nashville after graduating.
- **Cigna**, Actuarial Executive Development Program: priced Medicare Part C plans, then modelled
  pharmacy trend with formulary, FP&A, underwriting and clinical partners, including projections
  for GLP-1 drugs and for the end of the COVID public health emergency.
- **Fortitude Re**, life and annuity reinsurance: after three years in health, switched to a
  business with far more asset exposure and longer-dated liabilities. Built Python tooling for
  experience studies, then worked in planning and ALM, where the team produces the duration and
  convexity targets the investment side uses to size its swap hedges. The material in this lecture
  comes from that work.
- **Credentials**: ASA (2022), FSA (2024); passed CFA Levels I and II, Level III candidate.

One lesson for students: you are not stuck in the sector you start in.
